# 🍊 When Life Gives You Tangerines
## 🎥 Overview
*When Life Gives You Tangerines* is an upcoming **South Korean romantic drama** film directed by **Lee Hyori** and starring **IU** (*Lee Ji-eun*) and **Park Bo-gum*.  Set in the beautiful Jeju Island countryside, it tells a heartfelt story about growing up, friendship, and love amidst the scent of fresh tangerines.
